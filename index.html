About the Department

Established in 1996, the Department of Computer Engineering at SAKEC is NBA Accredited and affiliated with the University of Mumbai. The department aims to produce competent and ethical computer engineers equipped with strong fundamentals and industry-relevant skills in Software Development, Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Computing, and Cybersecurity.
