Department Vision

To become a globally recognized center of excellence in Computer Engineering education and research, producing technologically competent, innovative, and ethically grounded professionals who contribute meaningfully to societal progress.

Department Mission

  • Deliver rigorous, experiential, and industry-aligned computing education through state-of-the-art curricula and pedagogical practices.
  • Establish modern laboratory infrastructure to foster high-impact research, innovation, and technological entrepreneurship.
  • Cultivate ethical integrity, environmental consciousness, lifelong learning aptitude, and collaborative leadership among students.
